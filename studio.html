      On-Device Storage

      Photos you upload here are kept in this browser's own storage and are never sent to a server. Manufacturer images remain the property of their respective owners; a photo you add is published under your own responsibility.

      Framing Standards

      All display frames use standardized 8:5 proportions. Zoom, focus and mode choices stay on this device, and the catalogue page in this browser reflects your framing immediately.

      Export your photos and run the bake script to update the repository data. VahanaIQ is an independent personal project.

      Photo Studio

      Export My Photos

        What to do with the file
        1. Keep the download beside the working files (exports are not committed).
        2. From the project folder run node scripts/apply-studio-photos.mjs vahanaiq-studio-photos-DATE.json. It writes the framed photos into assets/images/curated-catalogue/ and updates data/catalogue.json and js/catalogue-data.js together, so the two can never drift.
        3. Review the change, then commit and push. The published catalogue will then show your photos, your order and your cover.

        Published photos you removed are left out of the export; the rest of the entry's prices are untouched.
