How Rentals Are Worked Out

The sum behind every monthly figure.

The Calculation

A rental is the amount financed multiplied by the monthly interest rate, divided by one minus (1 + monthly rate) to the power of minus the number of months. The monthly rate is the yearly reducing-balance rate divided by twelve. A flat rate quoted by a lender is converted to its reducing-balance equivalent first. Down payments follow the Central Bank of Sri Lanka minimums in Directions No. 01 of 2026 for the vehicle’s condition and class. These are estimates; your lender’s quote decides.
