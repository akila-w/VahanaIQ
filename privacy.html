VAHANAIQ · INFORMATION

Privacy

Know what stays on your device and what leaves it.

Guest use

The current app stores your loan settings, comparison, price edits, theme and savings goals in this browser’s local storage. This data persists until you clear it or use the relevant removal controls. The service worker caches this site’s files for offline use.

The Photo Studio

Photos you upload, frame or choose as a cover in the Photo Studio are kept in this browser’s own database (IndexedDB) on this device. They are never sent to a server or synchronised to your account, and the AI service never sees them. Undo My Changes and Remove all studio photos delete them, as does clearing this site’s browser storage. Publishing a change to the live catalogue is a separate step on your own computer, using node scripts/apply-studio-photos.mjs with the exported file.

Optional Google sign-in

Signing in uses Google authentication. Your loan settings, income, commitments, comparison and saved savings goals are synchronised to your private user document in Firebase. Images created in this browser are excluded from the synchronised copy. The app checks for conflicting edits before replacing data.

Signing out and deleting data

Signing out normally clears the account’s app data from this browser. If changes have not synchronised, the app keeps those local edits and shows a warning so they are not lost. “Delete my saved data” requests removal of the app’s saved Firebase document; it does not delete your Google account. Clearing browser storage separately removes guest data and the offline cache.

AI searches and uploads

When you use AI search, the entered query, pasted advertisement text and any attached screenshot are sent through the VahanaIQ search service to Google Gemini to generate a result. Remove personal information from advertisements and images before submitting them. AI search is optional; browsing and manual entry do not require it.

Sharing

A comparison link contains your selected vehicles, prices, conditions and loan settings. Income and existing payments are excluded unless you explicitly choose to include them. Anyone with the link can read what it contains. Review it before sharing.

External services

Remote vehicle images, source links, Google authentication, Firebase and AI services connect to their respective providers. Normal connection information may be received by those services. See Google’s privacy policy for Google services. The current app has no advertising or analytics trackers.

Questions

Use the public issue tracker for general privacy questions only. Do not include private financial or account data.
