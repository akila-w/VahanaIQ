# VahanaIQ catalogue and website review

Reviewed 21 Oct 2026. This is the current product/data review; the older photo review and research pages are retained as historical working material.

## Ready to review

- 191 entries across 144 model groups and 29 makes, with detailed trim levels, pricing and technical specifications (powertrain, output, transmission, drivetrain, fuel economy/range, dimensions, ground clearance, seating, safety and key features) on every vehicle card.
- 2,555 official-page photo candidates across all 144 models, with the official web listing hero image (`isHero: true`) set as the default card cover and full gallery controls to override or export choices.
- Photographs use 8:5 frames. Small 640 × 400 WebP previews preserve the source proportions; originals remain available for inspection.
- Brand marks, available genuine model wordmarks and Lucide glyphs replace some repeated text.

## Website changes

- Mobile navigation, collapsible loan terms, readable inputs, 44-pixel primary controls and responsive comparison cards. No page-width overflow at 320, 390, 430, 768, 1,024 and 1,440 CSS pixels in the browser checks. This is not a complete accessibility certification or a real-device test.
- Bulk add applies to all catalogue, search or filtered matches across pages, skips invalid/unpriced/conflicting entries and avoids duplicates. Clear and Undo are available. Large comparisons show 24 rows at a time.
- Affordability filtering uses entered income, commitments, interest, term, insurance and the vehicle’s required deposit. Only substantiated prices qualify for the catalogue’s within-budget filter; a lender’s approval is separate.
- Savings goals support a deposit or cash target, current savings, fixed/percentage monthly contributions, a target date, saved goals and optional account synchronisation. No future price growth or investment return is assumed.
- New/recent passenger vehicles require at least 60% down; eligible older locally registered or commercial vehicles use 40%. A 40% maximum loan is a 60% minimum deposit. Pickups and vans retain conservative assumptions until their registration class is confirmed.
- Calculations state which user inputs they use. Price evidence and fixed legal/product limits are labelled separately. Friendly dates, personal copyright and About, Privacy and Terms pages are included.

## Review limitations

Official provenance does not prove the photograph matches the exact generation, market or trim, or grant an unrestricted reuse licence. Every model group now has at least one official-source candidate image, though several grey-import or older models rely on official brochure or global press assets. Choose covers only after visual inspection. The Toyota Yaris Cross records include different market families and require particular care.

Google/Firebase payload compatibility is covered by local tests. Live sign-in and cross-device synchronisation were not exercised. The website has not been deployed or pushed as part of this review.
