# Sri Lankan passenger-car and SUV catalogue research

Updated: 20/10/2026. First implementation batch: **146 entries across 27 makes** in `js/market-catalogue.js`. This is a documented market sample, not a verified inventory of every vehicle or historical generation in Sri Lanka.

## Evidence rules

Local agent range pages establish that a model is marketed; they do not guarantee current stock, exact specification, warranty or a price. Importer listings, previous deliveries, local brochures and local configurators carry separate status labels. Model-range entries have null trim/year/powertrain where evidence is insufficient. User-entered quotes are separate from published amounts.

Eight entries currently carry a numeric price: three unambiguous Nissan Magnite variants, three Mahindra XUV 3XO starting prices and the two Leapmotor C10 REEV trims. Other entries require a user quote before lease calculations. Nissan's turbo Tekna+ page shows multiple prices; Perodua's search index showed amounts while the fetched page showed 00.00 placeholders. No ambiguous or placeholder amount was copied. Source-check date is not a publication date.

## Exclusions and holds

- Out of scope: Toyota Alphard, Hilux, Lite Ace, Hiace and Coaster; Suzuki Ertiga and XL6; Nissan Gravite and Urvan; Kia Carnival, Carens/Clavis and Tasman; BYD M6 and Shark 6; Renault Triber; Perodua Alza. MPV/cab/van classification takes precedence over an overly broad local navigation label.
- Honda's [official range](https://www.honda.lk/) places coming-soon labels on automobile entries. These are not copied as confirmed current agent stock; independent Vezel/WR-V importer evidence is separate.
- [BMW's current local landing page](https://www.bmwsrilanka.com/) announces a new importer chapter without a model range. A current independent used-car listing supports the single BMW entry; new-agent variants remain pending.
- [Mercedes-Benz's model menu](https://www.mercedes-benz.lk/models/) says available soon, while some detail pages display prices. Current agent-order status must be reconciled before publishing those prices. The existing independent E200 listing is separate.
- GWM Ora is marked coming soon on [the local range page](https://www.gwmsrilanka.lk/); do not publish it as current stock.
- Roomy, Every Wagon, Spacia and Wagon R Smile require consistent body-classification review before inclusion; a dealer calling all of them passenger cars is insufficient to settle the requested MPV/van exclusion.
