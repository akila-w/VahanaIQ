Terms and Notices

Planning estimates with a visible basis.

Planning tool, not a quote

VahanaIQ’s outputs are indicative calculations from your inputs. They are not a credit offer, lender approval, guaranteed valuation or financial, legal, tax or investment advice. Confirm a written quote, all fees and eligibility directly with the relevant provider before making a commitment.

Calculation assumptions

Lease estimates use monthly payments and reducing-balance calculations; flat rates are converted for the chosen term. Insurance and fees use the amounts you enter. Savings goals assume regular monthly contributions, no investment growth and no future vehicle-price changes. Living costs, fuel, maintenance, early settlement and other charges are included only where explicitly stated.

Down payments and budget limits

The current down-payment engine follows clause 2.1 of CBSL Directions No. 01 of 2026 for passenger vehicles. Commercial D-class minibuses use a 40% minimum deposit; other new additions use the passenger/B-class 60% planning basis unless registered locally for more than a year. Pickups and vans need their registration class confirmed with the lender. A lender may apply a different valuation or additional requirements. The chosen debt-to-income percentage is a planning limit, not a claim that CBSL guarantees affordability. The 84-month tenure cap is this tool’s current product limit. Check the primary Direction and your lender’s current requirements.

Prices, availability and AI

A listing or range page does not establish live stock, the exact specification, registration age or final on-road cost. Published asking/starting prices, historical figures, user-entered quotes and unverified estimates are distinct. AI results can contain errors. Check the linked source and exact variant before relying on any figure.
