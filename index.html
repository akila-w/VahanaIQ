How Rentals Are Worked Out

Rentals use the standard reducing-balance formula with monthly compounding; a flat rate is converted to its reducing-balance equivalent. Insurance and fees are included only when you enter them; fuel, servicing and early settlement charges are not.

Down Payments

Down payments follow CBSL Directions No. 01 of 2026 (cl. 2.1). Lenders apply the limit to their own valuation of the car (the agent's confirmed value, the customs or dealer invoice, or a professional valuer's report), so your down payment can differ.

Source Guide

Images

Selected visually from manufacturer and authorised distributor websites. Sri Lankan sources are preferred. Manuals, promotional graphics, wrong models and repeated colour views are excluded. Every frame uses the same 8:5 proportion.

Prices

Distributor Price
A published Sri Lankan manufacturer or authorised distributor amount. Starting prices, offers and stock-specific prices retain their source conditions.
Dealer Asking Price
A local dealer’s offer for the stated vehicle, year and condition. It may differ from an official distributor price.
Published Launch Price
A dated manufacturer announcement. Introductory availability must be reconfirmed.
Estimated Price
An indicative market calculation based on customs tariff, CIF, and distributor bracket where a custom quote is required.
Price on Request
A matching numerical price has not been substantiated. No amount has been invented to fill the gap.
