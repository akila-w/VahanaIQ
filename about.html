About VahanaIQ

A clearer view of your next car.

Built for Sri Lankan car buyers

Browse cars, SUVs, MPVs, passenger vans, minibuses and pickups, compare lease estimates and plan a deposit using your own income, commitments and savings. Guest mode works without an account.

Your numbers, your decisions

Rentals and savings projections recalculate from the price, rate, tenure, deposit, income and other amounts you enter. Catalogue prices come from external sources; unverified estimates and starting prices are labelled. Your lender makes its own valuation and approval decision.

Independent and transparent

VahanaIQ is a personal project created by Akila Wickramasinghe, not a company. It is an independent planning tool. It is not a lender, broker, vehicle dealer or professional financial adviser, and is not affiliated with the Central Bank of Sri Lanka or the vehicle brands shown.

Feedback and corrections

Report a reproducible problem or a source correction through the project’s issue tracker. Do not post account identifiers, financial details, passwords or private screenshots in public issues.
